Observer: closed candidate set, unknown egress offset and drift, full observation, no payloads or dummy labels. Legacy matching uses the historical causal lag search. Aligned matching searches a bounded affine grid; offsets combine clock error and relay delay. Both use exact normalized histogram L1. Drift is a deliberately large stress test, not measured hardware behavior. Ties receive expected uniform top-one credit and midrank. Random-ranking is an analytical control; unmodified traces are a diagnostic control.

Real alignment runtime is preserved in measurements.json.gz and runtime.json. Generated inputs, raw event accounting, exact scores, hashes, and the protocol accompany this report. No network resources are required.
